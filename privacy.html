Who runs the site

Cassian is presented by Min10 Studios, the music brand of Min10Tech LLC. This website presents Cassian and browser-based utilities including ZeroTrace PDF. It has no checkout, advertising, or database for visitor messages. The local staging preview includes a test-only Cassian launch signup, described below.

Cassian Backer VIP List — staging test

The proposed optional list is for Cassian Kickstarter launch updates from Min10 Studios. Cassian is free; the future Kickstarter supports its development. Neither a pledge nor email signup is required to use Cassian. This local preview accepts only example.com, example.net or example.org test addresses with an unchecked-by-default consent box. It sends that test address and consent version to the loopback preview server for validation. The adapter does not save addresses, create subscribers, log request bodies or send email. Closing or reloading the page clears its form state. On a public host, the test form is unavailable.

There is no live email provider, subscriber list or unsubscribe service connected. Before a live signup opens, this notice must identify the selected provider, actual processing and retention, consent confirmation and unsubscribe/deletion routes. Joining the eventual list is optional and will not constitute a pledge, reserve a reward or guarantee a launch date. For a privacy question, contact contact@min10studios.com.

Connections to GitHub

The home page reads public release metadata from the fixed Cassian repository on GitHub. Your browser sends normal connection information, including your IP address and browser details, to the hosting provider and GitHub. Those providers may retain operational or security logs.

The public site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Browsing and downloading require no ChatGPT or GitHub sign-in. A GitHub account is needed only if you choose to submit an issue.

Social channels and email

Social icons are local artwork, not embedded feeds or tracking widgets. Following a social link opens that service under its own privacy policy. Email links open your mail app; messages you choose to send go to Min10 Studios through its email providers.

Your guitar session

The Cassian homepage does not connect to the Cassian app, inspect installed software, request camera or microphone access, or read recordings, rigs or files. ZeroTrace reads only the files you select for its local processing workflow, described below. The tuner pictured on the home page is an illustration; tuning happens in the app.

Suggestions you prepare

Suggestion fields stay in the page while it is open. This website does not save or automatically send them. Choosing “Review & submit on GitHub” opens a GitHub issue draft with your text in its URL. GitHub then receives that text; you decide whether to submit.

Leave out credentials, private contact details and private recordings.

Cookies and browser storage

Our website code sets no cookies and uses no local or session storage, service worker, analytics or tracking pixels. Hosting and authentication infrastructure operate under their providers’ policies.

Downloads and the clipboard

Installer downloads and source files come from GitHub Releases. Repository, license and issue links use GitHub’s services and policies. “Copy SHA-256” writes only the displayed digest to your clipboard when you press it.

ZeroTrace PDF and your documents

ZeroTrace PDF reads files you select into browser memory and processes them locally with JavaScript. Its processing code does not upload selected PDF bytes, filenames, or metadata reports to our servers. The page does not save PDF contents in cookies, local storage, or a server database.

Before reports can display sensitive metadata from the original file. Files and reports remain available in the open tab until you clear them or close it. Clearing the queue releases the application's references; it is not secure erasure of device memory. Downloaded PDFs and audit files remain wherever you save them and are subject to your browser, device, backup, and synchronization settings.

An audit is a local record of the supported checks. It can include a filename and detected properties, so review it before sharing. A successful check means no remaining fields were found in the defined metadata scope; it is not proof of complete anonymity, content redaction, or compliance with a legal requirement.

Advertising and future cookie use

Advertising is disabled in this version. The labeled spaces on ZeroTrace reserve room for possible future advertisements; no Google AdSense script or advertising cookie is loaded by this implementation.

If Google AdSense is enabled later, Google and other selected advertising partners may use cookies and similar identifiers to deliver, personalize, and measure advertising based on visits to this and other websites. Older disclosures may call Google's advertising cookie a DART cookie; advertising technologies also include other identifiers. These website and advertising requests are separate from local PDF processing.

Before advertising starts, we will update this notice with the actual providers and configure the applicable consent and opt-out controls. Google's own explanations are available in How Google uses advertising technologies and My Ad Center. Links to these controls do not replace a site's required consent mechanism.

Privacy choices

No advertising permission is requested or used in this ad-disabled version. If advertising is introduced, visitors will need a clear way to review choices, refuse optional processing, and withdraw consent. Applicable regional privacy rights and opt-out signals, including Global Privacy Control where required, will be considered in that configuration. A future consent interface will not affect whether ZeroTrace can process a PDF locally.

For a privacy question or a request concerning information you sent us by email, contact contact@min10studios.com. Describe your request without attaching confidential PDFs, credentials, or other unnecessary personal information.
