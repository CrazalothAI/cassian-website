TONE / AMP & PEDALS

Keep the rig in view.

The approved Tone layout brings the amp and inline pedal controls together. Adjust the controls on each pedal’s face, with the signal path organized before the amp and after the cabinet.

CHOOSE A SOUND

Five faces. Familiar presets.

Choose Ferrum, Bastion, Lumen, Rubicon or Viridian. Preset cards sit directly beneath the illustrated heads, ready to recall a complete rig.

Change the amp.
Keep your pedalboard.

“Choose amp” changes the head without replacing the pedalboard. Presets recall amp, cabinet and pedal settings as a complete rig, with Keep or Cancel for the audition.

Search stays with the chooser, so the next head or preset is close at hand.

The launch selection is limited to these five illustrated heads and their existing settings.

INLINE PEDAL CONTROLS

Reach for the pedal.

EQ sliders, delay controls and reverb controls stay on their illustrated chassis. The current design keeps labels, values and bypass controls with the pedal you’re editing.

Find it.
Place it.
Play it.

The visual pedal picker combines artwork, search and categories. Select an effect, choose an available position and add it to the board.

Illustrated amp heads show the approved Cassian artwork. UI previews, when shown, are labeled separately.

BAND / PRACTICE

Give the riff a rhythm section.

Play with drums and bass. Choose a groove, set the root key and tonality, and type a manual tempo from 40 to 240 BPM or tap it in.

Change the feel as you play.

Adjust key, mode, bass movement and groove while the Band plays. Musical changes take effect at the next bar; drum and bass levels respond immediately. Recording and preparation can temporarily lock controls.

Take the arrangement with you.

Export the saved Band arrangement as MIDI, with an optional matching WAV. Apply your arrangement edits before export, then use the completed files in your DAW.

LEARN / TONE CRAFTING ACADEMY

Learn the part.
Shape the sound.

Explore scales and chord shapes on the interactive fretboard, choose a lesson key, and work through genre pathways. The circle of fifths and transposed progressions keep the theory close to the guitar.

Try the tone.

Use the existing tone recipes to explore how amp, cabinet and pedal choices work together.

Play with Band.

Send the lesson’s available groove and key to Band, then press Play when you’re ready. Cassian asks before replacing a custom arrangement.
