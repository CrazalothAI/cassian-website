TUNE. MUTE. PLAY.

Get in tune.
Stay in the session.

A clear horizontal cents meter shows exactly where the note sits, from −50 to +50 cents. Calibrate the reference to 432 Hz, 440 Hz, 442 Hz, or the pitch your session needs.

Use Standard, Drop D, DADGAD and open tuning presets. Mute guitar monitoring instantly while you tune, then get straight back to playing.

Feel the pick.

The result is a smoother drive response that keeps the shape of your attack. Existing saved rigs retain their chosen sound version.

BEFORE YOU PLUG IN

A few practical things.

Do I need a DAW?

No. Open the standalone app to play and record. Use the VST3 plugin when you want Cassian inside a compatible Windows DAW.

What do I need to connect my guitar?

A Windows x64 computer, an audio interface with an instrument input, and headphones or speakers. Use the manufacturer’s ASIO driver when available.

Does Cassian need an internet connection?

The core engine runs offline. This website connects to GitHub to verify downloads; playing in Cassian does not require an online session.

Are the drums and bass included?

Yes. The free core includes the integrated Band screen, 15 genres and 75 grooves. You do not need a separate MIDI instrument plugin.

Is this a finished release?

Cassian 8.0 is a public beta. Keep copies of your sessions and share any reproducible problems through the issue tracker.
