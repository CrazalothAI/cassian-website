06 / BEFORE YOU PLUG IN

LESS SETUP.
MORE PLAYING.

Bedroom. Rehearsal room. Main stage.
Bring your guitar. We’ll bring the band.

Do I need an expensive audio interface?

No. Cassian runs on any standard USB guitar interface (Focusrite Scarlett, AudioBox USB, Volt, Behringer, M-Audio).

Does it run standalone, or do I need another DAW like Reaper or Ableton?

Both! You can launch Cassian directly from your desktop as a standalone app to jam in 3 seconds, or load it as a VST3 plugin inside any major DAW.

Does it require an active internet connection to play?

Never. All audio processing, neural models, and licensing run 100% offline. Take it on stage or into a cabin without Wi-Fi.

Is the drummer really free?

Yes. The Free Core includes the dynamic virtual session drummer and bassist so you never have to play to a boring metronome click again.
