Cassian includes eight selected mono 48 kHz / 24-bit PCM WAVs from the pack.
Each is byte-for-byte identical to its original source WAV and is renamed only:

Patch 1  CookieMonster -> Cassian_BG412S_V30_SM57.wav
Patch 12 WorldCollider -> Cassian_BG412S_V30_SM57_Alt.wav
Patch 9  DevilsCunnilingus -> Cassian_BG412S_V30_PDMIC75.wav
Patch 2  DarthGenocider -> Cassian_BG412S_DV77_SM57.wav
Patch 3  KittenSlayer -> Cassian_BG412S_Rockdriver_e606.wav
Patch 6  VerticalLipStabber -> Cassian_BG412S_DV77_V30_DualSM57.wav

Patch 8  BigBubba -> Cassian_BG412S_Rockdriver_V30_e606_SM57.wav

The source cabinet is a modified oversized Behringer BG412S. These are not
Mesa cabinet captures. Mic positions are not published, so Cassian does not
label these captures as cap-edge, cap or cone positions. Several selections
are blends authored in the original pack; Cassian creates no additional blend
and preserves the complete source tails. Source/delivered hashes and frame
counts are in assets/irs/manifest.json.

Microphone naming follows the handbook's patch table: PDMIC75. The technical
list instead says PDMIC78. This discrepancy is retained in the provenance;
Cassian does not independently resolve the microphone's exact model.

Equipment and product names identify the source recordings. Their use does not
imply endorsement by or affiliation with the manufacturers or the creator.
